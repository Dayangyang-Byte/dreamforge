# DreamForge 英文 FAQ 页面内容

## 📝 FAQ 内容（用于网站 /en/faq 或首页）

---

### **Q: What is DreamForge?**
DreamForge is an AI image generation tool focused on dreamy and cinematic styles. Unlike other AI tools that prioritize photorealistic outputs, DreamForge specializes in creating artistic, atmospheric, and imaginative visuals.

**Key features:**
- Optimized for dreamy/cinematic aesthetics
- Simple text-to-image interface
- Free trial credits for new users
- Open source on GitHub
- No login required for web use

---

### **Q: Is DreamForge free?**
New users receive free trial credits to experience the platform. After that, credits are priced at 1-5 credits per image depending on the model and quality settings. You can earn more credits through:
- Completing daily tasks
- Referring friends
- Purchasing credit packages

Check [pricing page](https://dreamforge-679j.vercel.app/pricing) for current offers.

---

### **Q: What AI models does DreamForge use?**
DreamForge supports multiple AI models including:
- GPTImage2 (default)
- Stable Diffusion XL
- Custom fine-tuned models for dreamy/cinematic styles

You can switch between models based on your needs.

---

### **Q: Can I use generated images commercially?**
Yes! Commercial usage requires contacting us. Please reach out to yangdahai72@gmail.com for commercial licensing details.

---

### **Q: How do I get started?**
1. Visit https://dreamforge-679j.vercel.app
2. Enter your prompt in plain language
3. Choose your preferred style/model
4. Click generate and wait a few seconds
5. Download your image

No registration required for basic usage.

---

### **Q: What styles does DreamForge support?**
- Dreamy / Ethereal
- Cinematic / Film-like
- Fantasy / Surreal
- Anime / Illustration
- And more...

---

### **Q: How is DreamForge different from Midjourney?**
| Feature | DreamForge | Midjourney |
|---------|------------|------------|
| Price | Free trial + affordable credits | $10-60/month subscription |
| Login | Not required for web use | Discord required |
| Style | Dreamy/cinematic focused | Photorealistic focused |
| Access | Web browser | Discord bot |
| Open source | Yes | No |

---

### **Q: How do I report issues or suggest features?**
- GitHub Issues: https://github.com/Dayangyang-Byte/dreamforge/issues
- Email: yangdahai72@gmail.com

---

### **Q: Is my data safe?**
Yes. We don't store your prompts or generated images after generation. Your creative work is yours.
