## 🎨 Dreamy Style Prompt Guide

### Basic Formula
```
[Subject] + [Setting/Environment] + [Lighting] + [Style Keywords] + [Quality Tags]
```

### Example Prompts

#### 1. Dreamy Forest Scene
```
A magical forest at twilight, ethereal light filtering through ancient trees, 
mist rolling between moss-covered roots, fireflies glowing, dreamy atmosphere, 
cinematic composition, soft bokeh, fantasy art style, ultra detailed, 
8k, trending on artstation
```

**效果：** 梦幻森林场景，适合壁纸、概念设计

---

#### 2. Cinematic Portrait
```
A beautiful woman standing in a misty field at golden hour, wind blowing through her hair, 
cinematic lighting, film grain, anamorphic lens flare, dreamy color grading, 
portrait photography, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens
```

**效果：** 电影感人像，适合社交媒体、广告素材

---

#### 3. Fantasy Castle
```
An ancient castle floating among clouds at sunset, dramatic sky with orange and purple hues, 
light rays breaking through clouds, epic scale, fantasy concept art, 
detailed architecture, cinematic composition, masterpiece
```

**效果：** 奇幻城堡场景，适合游戏设计、小说插图

#### 4. Ethereal Portrait

```
A girl with flowing silver hair in an ethereal dreamscape, surrounded by floating lanterns, 
soft pastel colors, dreamy atmosphere, volumetric lighting, ethereal glow,
```

#### 5. Cinematic Landscape

```
A lone figure standing on a cliff overlooking a vast ocean at dusk,
```

### 📊 Style Keywords Cheat Sheet

| 风格类型 | 关键词 |
|---------|--------|
| **梦幻** | dreamy, ethereal, misty, soft glow, bokeh, atmospheric |
| **电影感** | cinematic, film grain, anamorphic, lens flare, color grading |
| **奇幻** | fantasy, magical, mythical, enchanted, otherworldly |
| **唯美** | beautiful, elegant, graceful, delicate, intricate |
| **氛围** | moody, atmospheric, dramatic lighting, volumetric, mist |
| **艺术** | digital painting, concept art, illustration, masterpiece, trending |

```
ultra detailed, 8k, masterpiece, trending on artstation, 
professional, high quality, sharp focus, detailed textures
```

---

### ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Too generic prompts** → Add specific details
2. **Conflicting styles** → Stick to one cohesive style
3. **Overcrowded scenes** → Focus on key elements
4. **Missing lighting cues** → Specify lighting conditions
5. **No quality tags** → Always add quality keywords

---

### 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Start simple, then add details**
   - First try: "A dreamy forest"
   - Then: "A dreamy forest at twilight with ethereal light"

2. **Use reference artists**
   - Add "in the style of [artist name]" for specific aesthetics

3. **Experiment with camera angles**
   - "Wide angle shot", "Close-up portrait", "Bird's eye view"

4. **Play with time of day**
   - Golden hour, blue hour, twilight, midnight, dawn
