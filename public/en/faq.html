Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DreamForge AI Image Generator

What is DreamForge?

DreamForge is an AI image generation tool focused on dreamy and cinematic styles. Unlike other AI tools that prioritize photorealistic images, DreamForge specializes in creating ethereal, atmospheric, and movie-quality visuals.

How much does it cost?

DreamForge uses a credit system:

  • Forge Image: 1 credit per image
  • GPT Image 2 (1K): 2 credits per image
  • GPT Image 2 (2K): 3 credits per image
  • GPT Image 2 (4K): 5 credits per image
  • Nannabanan: 2 credits per image
New users receive free trial credits when they sign up!

What models are available?

DreamForge offers three AI models:

  • Forge: Fast and affordable, great for quick iterations
  • GPT Image 2: Higher quality with better semantic understanding
  • Nannabanan: Specialized model for unique artistic styles
Each model has different credit costs and quality levels.

What styles does DreamForge support?

DreamForge features several preset styles:

  • Dream Cinematic: Soft lighting, ethereal atmosphere, film-like quality
  • Anime Illustration: Japanese anime and manga art styles
  • Realistic Photography: Photorealistic portraits and scenes
  • Product Poster: Commercial product photography
You can also use Custom mode for complete creative freedom.

Can I upload reference images?

Yes! DreamForge supports uploading up to 5 reference images. The AI will analyze them and intelligently determine which aspects to reference (person, outfit, background, pose, style) before combining with your text prompt to generate the final image.

Is DreamForge open source?

Yes! DreamForge is an open-source project. You can view the complete source code on GitHub: github.com/Dayangyang-Byte/dreamforge

How do I get more credits?

You can recharge credits via WeChat payment. The minimum recharge is ¥1 (approximately $0.14 USD). After payment, credits will be added to your account within minutes.

Where can I find prompt templates?

Check our Prompt Guide page for popular template examples and writing tips: Prompt Guide

What image ratios are supported?

DreamForge supports multiple aspect ratios:

  • 16:9 (Landscape) - Great for wallpapers and presentations
  • 9:16 (Portrait) - Perfect for mobile wallpapers and social media
  • 1:1 (Square) - Standard for social media posts
  • 3:4 (Cover) - Ideal for magazine covers and posters
The AI can also automatically detect ratio from your prompt.
