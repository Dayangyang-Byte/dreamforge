Key Style Elements

Lighting Keywords

Use these to create atmospheric effects:

  • Volumetric lighting - God rays, light beams through fog
  • Rim lighting - Backlit edges, hair glow effect
  • Golden hour - Warm sunset/sunrise tones
  • Blue hour - Cool twilight atmosphere
  • Dramatic lighting - High contrast, moody scenes

Atmosphere Keywords

Add depth and mood to your images:

  • Ethereal - Otherworldly, delicate beauty
  • Enchanting - Magical, captivating
  • Surreal - Dreamlike, beyond reality
  • Ethereal mist - Soft fog, atmospheric haze
  • Bokeh - Soft background blur

Quality Tags

Include these for better results:

  • 8k, ultra detailed - High resolution
  • masterpiece - Exceptional quality
  • trending on ArtStation - Professional artistic style
  • cinematic color grading - Film-like color treatment
  • film grain - Analog photography texture

Advanced Techniques

Reference Image Workflow

Upload up to 5 reference images to guide the AI:

  • Upload a person photo → AI will reference face and identity
  • Upload an outfit photo → AI will apply clothing style
  • Upload a landscape → AI will match colors and atmosphere
  • Upload multiple → AI intelligently combines all elements

Ratio Selection Tips

  • 16:9 - Desktop wallpapers, presentations, cinematic scenes
  • 9:16 - Mobile wallpapers, Instagram stories, TikTok
  • 1:1 - Social media posts, profile pictures
  • 3:4 - Magazine covers, book covers, posters
